YouTube recommends uploading an 800 x 800 px image for your profile picture. It will render as a circle at 98 x 98 px, so make sure your logo mark is centered and readable at small sizes.
The recommended size is 2560 x 1440 px. However, the "safe area" for text and logos that will display correctly across all devices (mobile, desktop, TV) is the center 1546 x 423 px. Baranded's templates account for this safe zone automatically.
